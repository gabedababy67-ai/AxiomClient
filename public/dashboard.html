HELP

FAQ

Quick answers for common Axiom setup questions.

How do I get access?

Join the Discord, receive the required server role, sign in here, and redeem a valid Axiom license key.

Why does my download say I have no license?

Redeem your key first. The key must be active, not revoked, and linked to this Discord account.

What does the device reset do?

It clears the client ID stored on your Axiom license so you can bind the license again. It does not change or spoof Windows hardware identifiers.

Where does the client file go?

Put the Axiom JAR in the mods folder for the Minecraft/Fabric version your build targets.
